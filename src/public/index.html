                  Tasks in the done lane (the last lane) are archived this many days after their completed date. They keep their completed date and stay in the done lane — click the archive-box icon in the lane's title row to show or hide them, or open one and click Unarchive. Archived tasks are also listed on the Archived tab.

                    A task pushed to KanDeck (API, n8n, scripts) without a board but with one of these tags goes straight into this board's first lane. Tasks with no matching tag still go to the Inbox. Each tag can belong to only one board; matching ignores upper/lower case. Tags save as soon as you click Add or ×.

                    When a task's tags match more than one board, KanDeck checks boards in rank order — rank 1, then 2, then 3 … — and sends it to the first board that owns any of its tags. A rank of 0 or blank means no rank: those boards are checked last. Boards with no rank, or the same rank, are checked in tab order. Saves as soon as you change it.
